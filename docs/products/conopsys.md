# ConOpSys

[conopsys.ai](https://conopsys.ai)

## What it does

ConOpSys is a generative AI platform for go-to-market intelligence. It converts raw source material — documents, interviews, meetings — into structured, scored insight: Knowledge Units extract competency frameworks from documents using BARS (Behaviorally Anchored Rating Scale) rubrics, Research Studies turn interview data into FIRE-scored themes with comparative analysis, and Retrospective Analysis rolls lessons learned across multiple meetings into patterns and actionable outcomes.

## Why we built it

GTM and revenue teams generate huge amounts of qualitative material — call notes, interviews, retros — that mostly gets read once and forgotten. We built ConOpSys to make that material structured and reusable: intelligence infrastructure instead of a pile of transcripts.

## How clients use it

- Sales and marketing teams turn competitive and market intelligence into structured frameworks instead of scattered notes
- Teams running customer or user interviews convert raw sessions into comparable, scored themes
- Revenue teams document lessons learned across meetings and surface recurring patterns instead of relearning them each cycle
