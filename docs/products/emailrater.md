# Vantage Mail (Email Rater)

[emailrater.cortadogroup.ai](https://emailrater.cortadogroup.ai)

## What it does

Vantage Mail grades cold outreach emails to PE firms and portfolio companies against your firm's own writing standards. Pick a target persona, submit a draft, and it's scored by AI across six dimensions — persona fit, tone & voice, clarity & structure, readability, persuasion, and CTA quality — plus a rule-based deliverability check.

## Why we built it

Outreach quality was inconsistent because there was no systematic way to check a draft against a professional bar before it went out — everyone eyeballed their own emails, and standards drifted rep to rep. We built Vantage Mail because the data shows AI-edited hybrid emails outperform both pure-AI and pure-human drafts, while pure-AI emails carry roughly double the spam-flag rate — and because deliverability depends more on sender reputation and authentication than on whether the email "sounds AI."

## How clients use it

- Reps submit a draft against a chosen reader persona and get a score across all seven dimensions before sending
- Teams train the scoring model against their own firm's writing corpus, so "good" means what your firm's best writers actually produce
- Outreach that "sounds like your firm" clears the bar before it reaches a partner's inbox
