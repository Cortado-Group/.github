# Level Up Quiz

[levelupquiz.ai](https://levelupquiz.ai)

## What it does

Level Up Quiz turns expert knowledge into interactive, AI-scored assessments that generate qualified leads. A creator's framework becomes a quiz; participants complete it, get a personalized maturity diagnosis and development plan, and the creator gets a validated, contact-verified lead.

## Why we built it

Traditional lead forms and generic gated content don't give prospects anything back, and they don't tell you much about who's actually a fit. We wanted a mutual value exchange: the prospect gets a real, personalized read on where they stand, and the creator gets discovery-quality intelligence before the first call — replacing a chunk of the manual discovery process outright.

## How clients use it

- **Consultants** pre-qualify prospects before the first consultation
- **PE/VC firms** run the assessment across portfolio companies to flag which ones need operational support
- **Authors and thought leaders** turn a published framework into an interactive experience
- **SaaS companies** qualify leads by maturity stage instead of a generic contact form
- **Sales teams** use it to gather discovery intelligence ahead of outreach

## In numbers

- 10,000+ assessments delivered
- 2,400+ qualified leads generated
- 94% completion rate
- Under 8 minutes average completion time
