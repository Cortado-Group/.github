# SDR Prep

[sdr.cortadogroup.ai](https://sdr.cortadogroup.ai)

## What it does

SDR Prep turns a company name and contact into a research-backed call kit in about 20 seconds: a prospect dossier, a personalized voicemail script, a follow-up email, and objection-handling talking points, all matched against the rep's target persona and pitch.

## Why we built it

Pre-call research is the first thing that gets skipped under quota pressure, and generic AI writers improvise rather than research — producing confident-sounding material that isn't grounded in the actual prospect. We built SDR Prep to compress 20 minutes of manual research into 20 seconds without losing the grounding, with particular attention to voicemail strategy since roughly 95% of cold calls hit voicemail and the transcript often gets read before the recording is heard.

## How clients use it

- Reps pull a dossier, voicemail script, email, and objection list before every call instead of winging it
- Reps queue and rank prospects by fit and buying intent
- Managers get visibility into rep activity, call dispositions, and prospect queue management through team analytics
- Teams configure target personas and territory rules so materials stay consistent across the floor
