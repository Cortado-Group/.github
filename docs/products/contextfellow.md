# ContextFellow (Fellow)

[contextfellow.ai](https://contextfellow.ai)

## What it does

Fellow is a document intelligence platform that answers questions from your uploaded business documents — contracts, call transcripts, emails, decks — and grounds every answer in a citation back to the source. If it can't point to where an answer came from, it says so instead of guessing.

## Why we built it

Standard AI chatbots are confident even when they're wrong: wrong buyer identified, a contract clause invented, stale information presented as current. Every one of those mistakes meant someone had to re-read the original PDF to check the AI's work, which defeats the purpose. Fellow was built to remove that verification step by refusing to answer past what the documents actually support — and surfacing the gap as the next thing to go find, rather than filling it with something plausible-sounding.

## How clients use it

- Sales teams verify account details before client calls instead of trusting an AI summary
- Teams build org charts, deal stages, and buyer personas grounded in actual communications, not inference
- AI agents pull account data through Fellow to avoid propagating hallucinated facts downstream

**Workflow:** upload documents once → extract structured claims → access with proof → share verified insights, with any claim clickable back to its source.
