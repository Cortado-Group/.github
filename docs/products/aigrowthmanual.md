# AI Growth Manual

[aigrowthmanual.com](https://aigrowthmanual.com)

## What it does

AI Growth Manual is a playbook — book, self-assessment quiz, and frameworks — for systematizing go-to-market strategy with AI, written by David Russell and Dan Bernoske of Cortado Group. It maps organizations onto five growth-maturity levels (Guesser, Manager, Analyzer, Systematizer, Enabler) and nine operational psychology personas, then walks through the frameworks to move up a level.

## Why we built it

We kept seeing the same pattern: companies hit "accidental success," can't explain why it worked, and then can't repeat it — unreliable forecasts, constant firefighting, data nobody trusts, more effort for diminishing returns. The manual exists to turn that reactive, heroics-driven mode into documented, automated systems.

## How clients use it

- Take the self-assessment quiz to identify their current growth-maturity level
- Work through the frameworks covering the five maturity stages and automation quadrants
- Use the persona analysis to understand the operational psychology at play on their team
- Read the 15-chapter guide for the practical systems and case studies behind each stage
