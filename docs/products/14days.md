# 14 Days to AI Readiness

[14days.cortadogroup.ai](https://14days.cortadogroup.ai)

## What it does

A 14-day, self-paced curriculum that takes a team from an honest AI-maturity assessment to a working plan. Day 1 is a reality check — an audit of what AI tools are actually in use, where the team sits on a five-level maturity spectrum (Ad-hoc → Experimenting → Operationalizing → Scaling → Transforming), and the gaps between current and target state — with each following day building on it through structured lessons, tasks, and reflection questions.

## Why we built it

Most organizations either overestimate or underestimate their own AI readiness, and both mistakes are expensive: skipping the assessment phase burns an estimated 40% of AI budget on misaligned initiatives. We built this as a structured, day-by-day way to get an honest baseline before spending on tools or headcount.

## How clients use it

- Complete daily lessons and tasks (e.g. inventory every AI tool in use, identify manual processes that could be automated, interview teammates about their own AI usage)
- Work through key questions each day to document current state, biggest barriers, and quick wins
- Use the maturity-spectrum placement to align leadership on where the organization actually stands before setting a roadmap
