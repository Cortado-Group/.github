# Cortado CRO Audit

[landingpage.cortadogroup.ai](https://landingpage.cortadogroup.ai)

## What it does

Cortado CRO Audit analyzes a landing page and tells you exactly why visitors leave without converting. Paste a URL for a free instant scan (11 mechanical checks: H1 count, HTTPS, viewport tags, response time — no rendering, no AI), then unlock a full audit that renders and screenshots the page and scores it against 75 conversion-focused criteria, with fixes ranked by expected impact and effort.

## Why we built it

Indie SaaS founders and marketers usually know *that* their landing page underperforms, not *why*. We built this to replace guesswork with research-backed, specific answers — the kind of feedback a conversion consultant would give, without waiting for one.

## How clients use it

- Run the free scan first to catch mechanical issues (broken viewport tags, missing HTTPS, slow load times) at no cost
- Unlock the full audit (credit-based, no subscription) for a scored breakdown against 75 criteria
- Use the prioritized fix list — or generate a rebuilt version — before investing time in a manual rewrite

Best suited to teams that want specific, actionable feedback fast; not a substitute for full agency partnership or guaranteed-traffic engagements.
