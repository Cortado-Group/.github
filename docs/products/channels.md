# Channel Partner Enablement OS

[channels.cortadogroup.ai](https://channels.cortadogroup.ai)

## What it does

Channel Partner Enablement OS guides channel partner reps through a three-step workflow — learn updated messaging, prepare a conversation with prospect context, handle objections safely — with every AI response constrained to language your legal and marketing teams have already approved. It also intake-scores incoming deals against criteria like funding, buyer access, and spend level before they reach the sales queue.

## Why we built it

Channel-led sales has a structural risk: partner reps sell outdated narratives, can't handle objections cleanly, and submit low-quality deals that waste internal resources — and because they're not employees, you can't fix it with a training deck alone. We built this so every partner conversation stays on-brand and legally sound without slowing partners down, and so weak deals get filtered before they cost internal time.

## How clients use it

- Partner reps walk the guided workflow to stay current on messaging and prep for a specific prospect
- Objection handling is scripted from approved knowledge, not improvised
- Different partner types (benefits platforms, carriers, brokers) get different messaging rules without any code changes
- Deals get scored on intake before reaching the sales queue, converting a conversation into a qualified opportunity in roughly 4 minutes
- A dashboard shows which partners are engaging, what objections keep recurring, and where knowledge gaps are — with white-label, partner-specific branding
