# Innovation Studio

[innovationstudio.cortadogroup.ai](https://innovationstudio.cortadogroup.ai)

## What it does

A 12-week, four-module curriculum that takes "smart and curious" participants through shipping real software and taking a product from idea to market, under mentor supervision: Python/SQL/OOP fundamentals (weeks 1–3), prompt engineering and AI integration (weeks 4–6), Kanban/testing/peer review (weeks 7–9), and MVP scoping through a capstone pitch (weeks 10–12).

## Why we built it

We believe the best way to learn is to do real work under real conditions, with people who care whether you grow — so the program is built around deliverables and mentorship rather than lectures and theory.

## How clients use it

- Participants progress sequentially through the four modules over 12 weeks, each with lessons, quizzes, assignments, and reference media
- They leave with a portfolio of completed projects, a working capstone product they scoped and built themselves, and working knowledge of AI in the product-development loop
- Ongoing mentorship relationships and cohort badge achievements carry past the 12 weeks
